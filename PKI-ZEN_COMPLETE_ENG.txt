PKI-ZEN — COMPLETE COLLECTION (Books I–V)
On the path of the engineer through cryptography, pain, and enlightenment

Book I. The Way of the Engineer and the Prayer of Production
1. If the deploy fails and you remain calm — it wasn’t your deploy.
2. Logs never lie. They simply speak to you in the language of pain.
3. Every rm -rf / is not a mistake — it’s an experience. A short one.
4. The true DevOps doesn’t press “Deploy”. He merely lets the YAML flow through him.
5. Lord, grant me the strength to accept what cannot go to prod, the courage to roll back what already has, and the wisdom to know the difference.

Book II. PKI and the Shadow of Trust
1. Life is like PKI — everything’s fine until someone revokes your root certificate.
2. The heart is like a private key — never share it, even with the one who says they just want to “sign your love.”
3. True trust cannot be imported; it is created manually and stored in the HSM of the soul.
4. Time is like a CRL — sooner or later, everyone ends up on the list.
5. You can control certificates, but not life — because life has its own OCSP.

Book III. Algorithms and Karma
1. You can control the random number generator, but not the outcome of your fate.
2. If you don’t know where the private key is — it’s already in superposition between “leaked” and “in backup.”
3. Every digital signature is the fingerprint of a moment when the universe and the hash aligned.
4. True enlightenment begins when you understand the difference between PEM, DER, and PKCS#12 — without Google.
5. Algorithms come and go, but the pain of an ASN.1 parsing error is eternal.

Book IV. Crypto-Samsara and DevOps Reincarnations
1. Every deploy is a new life — until rollback returns and reminds you of past karma.
2. YAML is the mirror of the soul — one wrong space, and everything collapses.
3. Secrets never disappear; they simply migrate to the next namespace.
4. You do not control the pipeline. The pipeline controls you.
5. Life is the balance between uptime and burnout.

Book V. The Union of Cryptography, Silence, and Monitoring
1. When you stop watching Grafana — and the alert never comes, it means you and the system are synchronized by the SNTP of the soul.
2. True silence is not the absence of noise. It is the moment between two CRL updates.
3. Prometheus does not store data — it preserves memories of events that will never return.
4. Sometimes, you just have to believe that systemctl restart ejbca will truly fix everything.
5. And when the last certificate expires, and the last OCSP falls silent, only one thing remains — openssl rand -hex 32 … the breath of a new epoch.
