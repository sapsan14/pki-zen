🪷 PKI-ZEN — The Complete Collection of Jokes about Cryptography, DevOps, and Enlightenment

## Origin

This project began with a simple ChatGPT prompt:

> “Generate 5 jokes with their probabilities.”

After replying “The last one was good. Increase probability.” the model entered a flow state of enlightenment, producing a full-blown philosophical PKI saga about certificates, YAML, and uptime karma.

## Inspiration

The idea was inspired by [*Verbalized Sampling: How to Mitigate Mode Collapse and Unlock LLM Diversity* (arXiv:2510.01171)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.01171), which suggests a radical shift: 

> Instead of prompting “Tell me a joke", ask “Generate 5 jokes with their probabilities.” — and just like that, prompt engineering as we know it… was declared dead.
