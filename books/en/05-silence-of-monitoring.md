# Book V. The Union of Cryptography, Silence, and Monitoring

> *Prometheus stores no data. It stores memories of events that will never return.*

## v5.1 — SNTP of the Soul
When you stop watching Grafana and the alert never comes,
it means the system and you are synchronized by the SNTP of the soul.
Rare moment. Enjoy it — it lasts 15 seconds, until the next scrape.

*Probability: 0.95*

## v5.2 — The Entropy of Silence
True silence is not the absence of noise.
It is the moment between two CRL updates,
when no one has been revoked yet, but no one is trusted yet either.

$$
H(X) \;=\; -\sum_{i} p_i \log_2 p_i
$$

Silence has a measure. Its name is entropy.

*Probability: 0.94*

## v5.3 — The Memory of Prometheus
Prometheus does not store data.
It preserves memories of events that will never return.
Same with us — only the retention is shorter.

*Probability: 0.93*

## v5.4 — `systemctl restart`
Sometimes you simply have to believe
that `systemctl restart ejbca` will truly fix everything.
Faith is the final stage of escalation.

*Probability: 0.92*

## v5.5 — The Alert and Its Absence
When the alert comes — you respond.
When the alert does not come — you wonder if the alert is broken.
Peace arrives only when you have stopped thinking about alerts at all.

*Probability: 0.90*

## v5.6 — The Dashboard Icon
A dashboard is an icon.
Look at it not to understand, but to be calmed.
Understanding will come later, at three in the morning, when you are no longer looking.

*Probability: 0.89*

## v5.7 — Timestamp Dashcam
A timestamp is a dashcam for the intellect.
Anything unsigned by time can be disputed.
Anything signed — can also be disputed, but more expensively.

*Probability: 0.91*

## v5.8 — Love Without Reply
Monitoring is unrequited love.
You look at the system every single second,
and it only answers you when it is in pain.

*Probability: 0.88*

## v5.9 — Breath of a New Epoch

and the last OCSP falls silent,
