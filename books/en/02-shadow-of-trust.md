# Book II. The Shadow of Trust and the HSM of the Soul

> *Trust is architecture, not feeling.*
> — from an observer's notebook ([life/reflect/2026-02-18_trust-field-theory.md](https://github.com/sapsan14/life))

## v2.1
Life is like PKI:
everything is fine until someone revokes your root certificate.
And it is, as a rule, not the enemy who revokes. It is the one who issued it.
*Probability: 0.95*

## v2.2
The heart is like a private key.
Never share it, even with the one who says they just want to "sign your love".
Real love will first present its certificate chain.
*Probability: 0.94*

## v2.3
True trust cannot be imported.
It is created manually and stored in the HSM of the soul —
the one even you connect to by PIN,
and which, from time to time, you forget.
*Probability: 0.96*

## v2.4
Time is like a CRL:
sooner or later, everyone ends up on the list.
While you are not there yet — go and sign.
*Probability: 0.93*

## v2.5
You can control certificates, but not life —
because life has its own OCSP.
It replies neither *valid* nor *revoked*, but *hold on, checking*.
*Probability: 0.92*

## v2.6
A true root CA does not shout about its authority.
It simply signs, and everything it signs lives.
Be like a root CA. But please, back it up.
*Probability: 0.90*

## v2.7
Do not trust the one who says "trust me" without naming *what result*.
Trust the result, not the session.
*Probability: 0.89*

## v2.8
A self-signed certificate is not a lie.
It is simply loneliness, formatted according to X.509.
*Probability: 0.87*

## v2.9
When a certificate expires, the world does not collapse.
The world collapses when you forgot to monitor it.
*Probability: 0.95*
